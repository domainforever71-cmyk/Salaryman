WHAT THIS IS

Salaryman is a browser-based career sim. You graduate at twenty, take a broker job, and have to survive on commission, weekly bills, and a boss who notices when you slack off. Everything is styled after old trading terminals and DOS-era hacker interfaces — dark screens, hard borders, monospace type.

It's a solo-built demo project, not a real brokerage or investment tool. Nothing inside it is financial advice.

THE GAME: ASTRA

Once you log in, you're dropped into ASTRA — the in-fiction terminal that runs the whole simulation. Cold-call clients off a directory of fifty-five procedurally generated investors and pitch them on your book. Interview at one of four real firms, from an entry-level shop to an elite partnership that only respects hard numbers. Trade five simulated markets against a live BTC/USDT feed. Or skip the ladder entirely and start your own firm.

Miss your targets too many times and your boss's mood runs out — you get fired, and you're back on the job market. Keep your head down and pull it off, and there's a leaderboard tracking who's actually good at this.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

LIVE, TALKING AI
Clients, interviewers, your boss, and four dial-pad bots (VEX, CHRONOS, NYX, GOLIATH) respond to exactly what you type — not a fixed script.
A REAL JOB MARKET
Four firms, four distinct interviewers, and a genuine hire-or-reject decision based on how you actually answer their questions.
YOUR OWN FIRM
Walk away from a salary and put up the capital to build a business that pays you passively instead of on commission.
A LIVE MARKET FEED
Real BTC/USDT pricing pulled in real time sits alongside the simulated stock, energy, and commodities desks.
CO-OP SYNDICATES
Found or join a room with a shared balance and shared trades, and run a firm together with other operators live.

ARCHITECT'S NOTE

Every line of this terminal — the interface, the game logic, the market simulation — was written by hand. No model touched the code.

But once you're inside, you're not doing this alone: VEX, CHRONOS, NYX, GOLIATH, your interviewers, and every client on the dial pad are live AI, reacting to whatever you actually say to them.
